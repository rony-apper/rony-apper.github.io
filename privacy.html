Privacy policy

Effective 1 October 2026.

SwimCalendarSync is used only by its owner's family. With your permission, it uses Google Calendar access only to create, update and delete swim events on calendars shared with it, and to read those calendars' events in the sync period.

No data is sold, shared with or sent to third parties. No analytics. Sign-in tokens are kept only in the owner's private storage.

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/rony-apper/rony-apper.github.io.
